Frequently Asked

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Upgrade" then "Restore Purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to your device Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel.

Is my data private?
Yes. Your data is stored in your own iCloud account and is never shared with third parties.
