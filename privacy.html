LogNutri Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

LogNutri collects the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Food and nutrition records you enter
  • Custom nutrients and foods you create
  • Nutrition goals you set
  • Purchase and subscription information (processed by Apple)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: displaying your nutrition records, tracking progress, and syncing across your devices via iCloud.

3. Data Storage and Sync

Your data is stored locally on your device and, if you enable iCloud, synced to your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. We do not have access to your personal iCloud data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data remains in your own iCloud account and on your device.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not collect or store your payment information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as you use the App. You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings → Reset All Data within the App.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since your data is stored in your own iCloud account, you can manage it through your device settings.
